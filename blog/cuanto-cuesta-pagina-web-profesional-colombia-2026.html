What drives the real price

1. Number of pages and sections

A one-page landing costs less than a site with home, services, about, blog, gallery and contact. More content means more design, development and review.

2. Custom design vs. template

A template is cheaper but looks like thousands of other sites. A custom design reflects your brand and sets you apart from competitors.

3. SEO included

This is what separates a website that generates customers from one nobody finds: speed, heading structure, metadata, schema and local SEO. To go deeper, see our SEO services for Miami and the USA.

4. Features and integrations

Bookings, payments, WhatsApp, forms, AI chat, an admin panel and multilingual support all add development hours.

5. Post-launch support

Many providers deliver and disappear. Ask what support, revisions and maintenance are included.

Watch out: if someone offers a 'professional' website for under $300, ask whether it includes responsive design, basic SEO, speed optimization and support. Often it includes none of them, and you end up paying twice to fix it.

What is different about Miami

Miami is a bilingual, tourism-driven and highly competitive market. That changes what your website actually needs:

  • A bilingual English/Spanish version with hreflang and keywords researched separately for each language.
  • Local SEO and Google Business Profile, key for 'near me' searches in Brickell, Wynwood, Doral, Coral Gables or Miami Beach.
  • Mobile-first experience: most local searches happen on a phone.
  • Web accessibility: in the USA it is wise to care for contrast, labels and keyboard navigation to reduce legal risk.
  • Fast conversion paths: call, WhatsApp, booking and form buttons visible from the first screen.

Freelancer vs. agency

  • Choose a freelancer if you only need a simple digital business card and do not expect to win customers from Google.
  • Choose an agency if you want the website to generate customers: that requires strategy, SEO, tracking and a proven process.

Hidden costs

Before signing, ask about these recurring costs:

  • Domain name: roughly $10–$20 per year.
  • Hosting and SSL certificate.
  • Premium plugins, business email and third-party tools.
  • Maintenance, updates and backups.
  • Ongoing SEO and advertising, if you want them.

Why is there such a big price gap between freelancers and agencies?

Lower-priced freelancers often deliver design only. Agencies usually bundle strategy, custom design, on-page SEO, responsive development, integrations and post-launch support.

Is it worth paying more for an agency instead of a cheap freelancer?

If your goal is a website that ranks on Google and generates leads, yes. A cheap site without SEO, speed optimization and a clear conversion path rarely produces customers, and fixing it later often costs more.

Do I need a bilingual website in Miami?
